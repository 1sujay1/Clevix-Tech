Transparent Exclusions Notice Across All Tiers

To maintain honest and affordable rates without hidden inflation, third-party recurring overheads are strictly excluded from development fees unless specifically invoiced as a managed addon:

  • Domain registration fees (.com, .in, etc.)
  • Web server and cloud hosting recurring bills
  • Direct Meta and Google ad spend paid to platforms
  • Third-party API subscriptions (SMS, WhatsApp API credits)
  • Google Play Store ($25) / Apple Developer ($99) accounts
  • Paid commercial fonts, stock photos, or premium plugin licenses
