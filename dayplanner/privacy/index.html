Privacy policy

Day Planner by Clevix Tech · Effective 17 November 2026

In short

  • You can use Day Planner without an account. Your tasks then stay on your phone.
  • With an account, we store what's needed to back up your tasks and share them with your family.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or from this website.

Who we are

Day Planner is an Android app made by Clevix Tech (clevixtech.com). For anything about your data, email info@clevixtech.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address and passwordWhen you create an accountTo sign you in and show your family who you are. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider in hashed form; we never see them.
Mobile numberWhen you create an account or add it in SettingsSo family can find and invite you by number.
Tasks you sync or share: title, notes, checklist, date and time, reminder, priority, category, done statusWhen you're signed inTo back them up and show them on the schedules of the family members you choose.
Family groups, members and invitations, including the mobile number you inviteWhen you invite or joinTo run family sharing.
A notification token for each phoneWhen you're signed inTo send instant notifications when family adds or changes a task for you.
Crash reports: what went wrong, phone model, Android version, app version and a random installation IDIf the app crashesTo find and fix bugs. They aren't linked to your account.

What stays on your phone

Tasks marked Personal, and all tasks when you're not signed in, are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Alarms are set on your phone. Backup files you export are saved wherever you choose.

Contacts

When you pick someone from your contacts to invite, Android gives the app only the number you picked. Day Planner does not read your address book. That number is sent to our server only to find or invite that person.

Who can see your data

  • Your family group sees your name, email address and mobile number, and the tasks on the schedules shared with them.
  • Someone who searches for your mobile number to invite you sees only your name and a partly hidden email (for example r•••@gmail.com). Searches are rate-limited.
  • Nobody else. Database rules allow access only to you and the members of your family groups.

Service providers

We use these companies to run Day Planner. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, sign-up emails and the cloud database.
  • Google Firebase: Cloud Messaging for notifications, and Crashlytics for crash reports.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The cloud database checks every request, so you can read and change only your own data and what your family shares with you.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account data until you delete your account.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on clevixtech.com/dayplanner/delete-account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, mobile number, the tasks on your schedule, the family groups you own, your invitations and your notification tokens. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. Tasks on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.

If Android backup is on, your phone may include Day Planner's local data in your own Google account backup. That backup is managed by you and Google.

Children

Day Planner is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your name and mobile number in Settings, export your tasks at any time, and delete your account. To ask for a copy of your data or for help with anything on this page, email info@clevixtech.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. For important changes we'll also let you know in the app.
