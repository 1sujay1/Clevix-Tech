FAQ

Common questions

Is Day Planner free?

Yes. Planning, alarms, family sharing and notifications are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. Day Planner works fully offline. Create a free account (name, email and mobile number) when you want to back up your tasks or share with family.

I installed an earlier test version

If Android says the app can't be installed, uninstall the old copy once and install this one. Tasks saved to your account come back when you sign in. Future updates install over this version.

Will alarms ring if my phone is locked?

Yes. Allow notifications and, if asked, "Alarms & reminders" and full-screen alerts. The Settings screen shows what's still needed.

Is there an iPhone app?

Android only for now.
