Delete your Day Planner account

Day Planner by Clevix Tech

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Day Planner and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email us from the address you use for Day Planner, so we know the request is really from you.

Email a deletion request

Or write to info@clevixtech.com with the subject "Delete my Day Planner account". We'll confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, password and mobile number
  • The tasks on your schedule stored in the cloud
  • Family groups you own, your memberships and your invitations
  • Notification tokens for your phones

This can't be undone. To keep a copy of your tasks first, use Settings → Export backup in the app.

What isn't deleted

  • Crash reports: they aren't linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Firebase within 90 days.
  • Tasks saved only on your phone (Personal tasks and backup files you exported): these are on your device. Uninstall the app or delete the files to remove them.
