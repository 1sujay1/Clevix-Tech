Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 06, 2026 • Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India

Note for Launch: This privacy policy reflects general principles under the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000 (and Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Review with a legal professional before production launch.

1. Introduction

Clevix Tech ("we", "our", or "us"), based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us through our website (clevixtech.com) and associated digital communications.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you or automatically collected during standard web interaction:

  • Inquiry Data: Your name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, service preferences, and project scope submitted through quote forms.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized browser type, operating system, IP address, and pages visited collected via privacy-focused analytics to improve site performance.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • Responding to service inquiries and preparing accurate project quotations.
  • Executing agreed client contracts, project deliveries, and invoicing.
  • Communicating project milestones, revisions, and technical updates.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may be shared with trusted infrastructure providers (such as secure cloud hosting and email service providers) strictly necessary to deliver our services.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures in accordance with the Information Technology Act 2000 to safeguard against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data stored with us. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@clevixtech.com.
