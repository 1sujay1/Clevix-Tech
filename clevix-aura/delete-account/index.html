Delete your Clevix Aura account

Clevix Aura voice assistant · App by Clevix Tech

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Clevix Aura app.
  2. In the account panel at the top, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — by email (without the app)

Email info@clevixtech.com with the subject Delete my Clevix Aura account, from the Google email you signed in with. We complete email requests within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Clevix Aura account: Google email, name and Google account ID
  • All signed-in sessions
  • Your daily command counts
  • Your subscription records held by Clevix Aura

What is kept

  • Nothing from your account. Clevix Aura does not store your recordings, conversations or screen content in the first place.
  • Payment records held by Google Play are kept by Google under its own policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Clevix Aura.
