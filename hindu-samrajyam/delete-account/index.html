Delete your Hindu Samrajyam account

இந்து சாம்ராஜ்யம் கணக்கை நீக்குதல் · App by Clevix Tech

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Hindu Samrajyam app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile (உறுப்பினர்).
  3. Tap Delete my account (கணக்கை நீக்கு) and enter your password.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — by email (without the app)

Email info@clevixtech.com with the subject Delete my account. Send it from your registered email, or include your member ID (for example HSR-012) and your registered phone number so we can confirm the account is yours. We complete email requests within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your membership profile: name, phone, email, address, district, ID proof number and profile photo link
  • Your login password and all signed-in sessions
  • Posts you published, and your event RSVPs
  • Reports you sent about other posts

What is kept

  • Contribution (donation) records are kept for up to 8 years where Indian accounting and political-funding laws require it, and are then deleted. They are visible only to party administrators.
