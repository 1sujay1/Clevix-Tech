Overview / சுருக்கம்: This Privacy Policy explains how Hindu Samrajyam ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, protects, and handles personal information when users access the mobile application, view official news, register as party members, generate digital ID cards, or participate in events.

1. Information We Collect / நாங்கள் சேகரிக்கும் தகவல்கள்

We only collect information strictly required to provide party membership, verify cadre identity, and deliver official news:

  • Personal Identity Data: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, District/City, State, and Residential Address (collected voluntarily during party membership registration).
  • Profile Photograph: Optional profile picture uploaded by the user specifically for the generation of the Digital Membership ID Card.
  • Identity Proof Details: Optional Voter ID / Aadhaar reference number for secretariat member verification.
  • Content you post: News posts, images and video links you publish, and reports you send about other posts.
  • Event RSVPs and contributions: Events you register for; the name, phone number and amount you enter when you record a contribution.
  • Settings stored on your phone: Notification preferences, language, dark mode, liked posts and blocked users. These stay on your device.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not collect your location or contacts.

2. How We Use Your Information / தகவல்களைப் பயன்படுத்தும் விதம்

Your information is used solely for official party operations and cadre communication:

  • To generate and issue authorized Digital Membership ID Cards.
  • To send official party news releases, conference schedules, and emergency breaking alerts.
  • To manage event RSVPs and coordinate district-level volunteer seva activities.
  • To authenticate administrators and members for authorized dashboard operations.
  • To review reported posts and keep the community safe.

Who Can See Your Information / யார் பார்க்க முடியும்

  • Public: Posts you publish (with your name and party role) and event details are visible to everyone who uses the app. Profile and post images are stored at public web addresses.
  • Party administrators only: Your phone number, email, address, ID proof number, event RSVPs and contribution details. Other members cannot see them.
  • Service provider: Data is stored with Supabase (cloud database and file storage), which processes it only to run the app.
  • We do not share personal data with anyone else unless required by law.

Community Guidelines / சமூக விதிகள்

By posting in the app you agree not to publish hate speech, content that promotes violence or discrimination against any religion, caste, community or person, harassment, sexual content, false or misleading information, or spam.

  • Any user can report a post (⋮ menu on the post). A post reported by several people is removed from the feed until an administrator reviews it.
  • Any user can block an author so their posts are no longer shown.
  • Administrators remove content that breaks these rules and may block accounts that repeatedly break them.

3. App Permissions / செயலி அனுமதிகள்

The app requests the following Android device permissions only when required:

  • Photo Picker / Storage: Uses the system zero-permission Android Photo Picker to select a profile picture for the digital ID card without broad storage access.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To deliver real-time news updates, press releases, and district event notifications. You can toggle preferences in Settings.
  • Internet (INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE): To fetch news articles, sync verified events, and verify member rosters.

4. Data Storage & Security / தகவல் பாதுகாப்பு

We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols:

  • All network communications are encrypted via SSL/TLS (HTTPS).
  • Passcodes and sensitive credentials are encrypted using secure hashing mechanisms.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, trade, or monetize member personal information to third parties or commercial advertisers.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion / பயனர் உரிமைகள் & நீக்கம்

Every registered member and guest has the right to:

  • View, edit, or update their membership profile and photo anytime in the Profile section.
  • Opt out of push notifications via the in-app notification toggles.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account. After you confirm your password, your membership profile, login, posts, event RSVPs and reports are permanently deleted from our servers straight away.
  • Delete without the app: Follow the steps at clevixtech.com/hindu-samrajyam/delete-account, or email info@clevixtech.com with the subject "Delete my account" from your registered email, or with your member ID and registered phone number. We complete the request within 30 days.
  • Contribution records are kept for up to 8 years where Indian accounting and political-funding laws require it, and are then deleted.

6. Age / வயது

Party membership is for people aged 18 and over. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.
