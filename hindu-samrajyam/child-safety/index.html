Child safety standards

குழந்தைப் பாதுகாப்புத் தரநிலைகள் · Hindu Samrajyam app by Clevix Tech · Last updated 29 October 2026

Hindu Samrajyam has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything in the app: news posts, images, video links, profile photos and any other content.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including images, video, text or links.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, or attempts to contact children for abuse.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material of any kind.
  • Content that promotes, normalises or threatens violence or harm against children.

Party membership and posting are only for people aged 18 and over.

How to report

  • In the app: open the post, tap the ⋮ menu → Report post → choose Child safety / child abuse (குழந்தைப் பாதுகாப்பு). A child-safety report removes the post from everyone's feed immediately while it is reviewed.
  • By email: info@clevixtech.com with the subject "Child safety report". Include the post title or a screenshot if you can.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call the police (112) or Childline (1098) in India first.

How we act on reports

  • Reported content is hidden at once and reviewed by the app team, usually within 24 hours.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed permanently, and the account that posted it is blocked.
  • Suspected CSAM is reported to the authorities: in India through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the police, as required by the POCSO Act 2012 and the IT Act 2000, and to other national authorities where required. We keep the evidence they need and do not share it with anyone else.
  • We cooperate fully with law-enforcement requests.
