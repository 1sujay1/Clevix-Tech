Clevix — Privacy Policy

Clevix voice assistant for Android · by Clevix Tech · Effective 19 November 2026

Clevix lets you control your Android phone by voice. This policy explains what the app hears, reads, sends and stores, and the choices you have. Clevix Tech ("we") operates the app and its cloud service.

The short version

  • The wake word "Hey Clevix" is detected on your phone.
  • What you say after waking it, and the screen text it needs for your command, are processed by our cloud and its AI and speech providers to carry out the command. We do not keep your recordings or conversations.
  • Clevix never opens or reads banking, UPI, wallet, trading or authenticator apps, never makes payments, and never reads or shares one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, card or account numbers.
  • We store only what accounts and plans need: your Google email and name, daily command counts, and your subscription status.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. What Clevix uses and why

DataWhenWhy, and where it goes
Microphone audio Always on in the background while Clevix is running (a notification shows this). Wake-word detection happens on the phone. When a sound is close to "Hey Clevix", the last 2–3 seconds are sent to check it. After waking, what Clevix hears until you say "stop Clevix" (or 2 minutes pass without a command) is sent to be turned into text. Audio is processed in memory and not saved by us.
Your spoken words (text)After waking Sent with the current conversation to our AI provider to understand the request and decide what to do. The phone keeps the conversation for a few minutes and then forgets it; our cloud does not store it.
Screen content of other appsOnly while carrying out a command that needs it, with Screen control (Android Accessibility) switched on by you The text and buttons of the app you asked about are read so Clevix can open, tap, type and scroll for you. That text is sent to the AI to decide the next step. Blocked apps (banking, UPI, wallet, trading, authenticators) are never read, and numbers that look like OTPs, PINs, cards or accounts are removed on the phone before anything is sent.
NotificationsOnly when you ask (for example "read my notifications"), with notification access switched on by you Read aloud to you. OTP, banking and payment notifications are hidden on the phone and never sent.
ContactsOnly when you ask to call or message someone The matching name and number are used to place the call or open the message, after you confirm.
Google account: email, name, Google account IDWhen you sign in To create your Clevix account, apply your plan and daily limit, and let you delete it.
Usage countsEach command Clevix acts on The number of commands per day, to apply your plan's daily limit. Not the content of the commands.
Subscription recordsWhen you subscribe on Google Play The Play purchase token, plan and expiry date, checked with Google, to give you the plan you paid for. We never see your card or bank details; Google Play handles payment.

Clevix does not request SMS, location, camera, call log or storage permissions, and does not collect them.

2. Who processes your data

We use these service providers only to run Clevix. They act on our instructions under their own terms and security commitments, and some process data outside India (for example in the United States).

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — understands your request and decides the next step.
  • ElevenLabs — turns your speech into text and Clevix's replies into speech.
  • Vercel — hosts the Clevix cloud service (Mumbai region).
  • Supabase — stores accounts, usage counts and subscription records.
  • Google — Sign in with Google and Google Play billing.

3. How long we keep it

  • Audio, transcripts, screen text and conversations: not stored by us; processed only to answer you.
  • Account, usage counts and subscription records: until you delete your account.
  • Basic server logs (time, error codes, response times — not what you said): up to 30 days, for security and fixing faults.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Stop listening any time with Stop in the app or notification; switch off Screen control and notification access in Android Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app (Delete account) or as described at clevixtech.com/clevix/delete-account. Cancel a subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to us at the address below.

5. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). AI and speech keys stay on our servers, never in the app. Sign-in sessions are stored only as one-way hashes. Purchases are verified with Google before a plan is granted. Access to the account database is limited to the Clevix cloud service.

6. Children

Clevix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date above and show the new notice in the app before it applies.
